Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 20, 2026

Friday Money respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our budgeting application.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Friday Money, we may collect:

  • Name and email address.
  • Bank account names, types, and last four digits.
  • Bank account balances.
  • Financial transactions, including deposits, purchases, withdrawals, and payments.
  • Budget preferences and application usage information.

2. Bank Account Connections

Friday Money uses Stripe Financial Connections to allow users to securely connect supported financial institutions. With your permission, we may access account balances, transaction history, and account details.

Bank connections are facilitated through Stripe. Friday Money does not directly collect your online banking password.

You control which accounts you connect and can disconnect accounts where supported.

3. How We Use Information

  • Display account balances.
  • Track income and expenses.
  • Organize financial transactions.
  • Create and manage budgets.
  • Provide spending summaries and financial insights.
  • Maintain and improve the application.
  • Protect accounts and prevent unauthorized activity.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal or financial information.

We may share information with service providers that help operate Friday Money, including Stripe, application hosting providers, and data storage providers. These providers may process information on our behalf to provide their services.

We do not share financial information with advertisers for targeted advertising.

5. Data Security and Storage

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal and financial information against unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

Access to information is limited to what is necessary to operate and maintain Friday Money.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain account functionality, and meet applicable legal requirements.

Users may request account deletion and removal of associated personal information by contacting us.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

7. Your Privacy Choices

You may choose which bank accounts to connect, disconnect linked accounts where supported, request access to your information, and request correction or deletion of personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Friday Money is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as Friday Money develops. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
